ReWildoku Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026 · Applies to ReWildoku version 1.4 and later on Android

In short: we (Latent Games) do not collect any personal information. There is no account and no sign-up, and your puzzle progress stays on your phone. ReWildoku offers optional rewarded ads from Google AdMob: you choose to watch one in return for a hint, a placement or an extra heart. Google may use your device's advertising ID to show and measure those ads, and in the EU, EEA, UK and Switzerland you are asked for your consent first.

Who we are

ReWildoku is made by Dean Galibert, trading as Latent Games, an independent developer based in County Waterford, Ireland ("we", "us"). Dean is responsible for this game and for this policy. If you have any question about this policy, email latentgamesstudio@gmail.com.

Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information. ReWildoku does not ask for your name, email address, phone number or any other personal details, and it does not contain analytics or crash-reporting tools. The only third-party service in the game is Google AdMob, described below.

The game saves the following on your own device only:

  • Game progress: the levels and habitats you have restored, your stars, daily puzzle times and streaks, and your hint and placement counts.
  • Settings and stats: your sound and display choices, and the numbers shown on the Stats screen.

This information never leaves your device and is never sent to us or to anyone else. You can play ReWildoku offline; an internet connection is only needed to watch an ad.

Rewarded ads (Google AdMob)

ReWildoku shows ads only when you choose to watch one, for example to get more hints, to place an animal, or to keep going with an extra heart. There are no banner ads and no ads that play without you asking.

The ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect and process information from your device, such as your device's advertising ID, IP address (used to estimate your general location), device and app information, and how you interact with the ad. Depending on your choices, the ads may be personalised. Google handles this information as described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy. We do not receive this information and cannot use it to identify you; we only see combined reports such as how many ads were shown.

Your choices: if you are in the EU, EEA, UK or Switzerland, ReWildoku asks for your consent before any ads are requested, using Google's consent form. You can change your choice at any time in the game under Settings, then Privacy choices. On any Android phone you can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your phone's settings (Settings, then Google or Privacy, then Ads). If you never watch an ad, no ad is loaded on your behalf beyond the consent check and Google's ad setup when the game starts.

Sharing your result

If you tap Share result, the game creates a short text summary of your daily puzzle (date, stars, time and streak) on your device. It is only sent to the app or person you choose, using your phone's own sharing options. We never receive it.

Third parties

Apart from Google AdMob (see above), ReWildoku does not include any third-party services that collect data. Google Play, the store you download ReWildoku from, may process information such as installs and app performance under Google's Privacy Policy. We only see anonymous, combined statistics from Google Play, such as the total number of installs.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your game data stays on your device until you delete it. You can erase it at any time:

  • In the game: Settings, then Reset progress.
  • On your phone: uninstall ReWildoku or clear its app data.

Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to delete on our side. To manage the data Google holds for advertising, use the advertising settings on your phone or visit My Ad Center.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, correct or delete personal data held about you, and to complain to a data protection authority. As we do not hold any personal data about ReWildoku players, we will normally have nothing to provide, but you are welcome to contact us at any time. You can also contact the Data Protection Commission, Ireland's data protection authority.

Children

ReWildoku is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly allow ads to be shown to children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of ReWildoku adds features that handle personal information, such as Google Play Games leaderboards, we will update this policy before that version is released, change the date at the top, and tell you in the game.
