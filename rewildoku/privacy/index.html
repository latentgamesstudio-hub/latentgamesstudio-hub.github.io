ReWildoku Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026 · Applies to ReWildoku version 1.0 on Android

In short: ReWildoku does not collect, store or share any personal information. There is no account, no sign-up, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your puzzle progress stays on your phone.

Who we are

ReWildoku is made by Dean Galibert, trading as Latent Games, an independent developer based in County Waterford, Ireland ("we", "us"). Dean is responsible for this game and for this policy. If you have any question about this policy, email latentgamesstudio@gmail.com.

Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information. ReWildoku does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, location or any other personal details, and it does not contain advertising, analytics or crash-reporting tools.

The game saves the following on your own device only:

  • Game progress: the levels and habitats you have restored, your stars, daily puzzle times and streaks, and your hint, undo and restart counts.
  • Settings and stats: your sound, theme and reminder choices, and the numbers shown on the Stats screen.

This information never leaves your device and is never sent to us or to anyone else. ReWildoku works fully offline.

Daily reminders

If you allow notifications, ReWildoku can remind you once a day that a new daily puzzle is ready. These reminders are scheduled on your phone by the game itself. No data is sent anywhere to make them work. You can turn them off in the game's settings or in your phone's notification settings.

Sharing your result

If you tap Share result, the game creates a short text summary of your daily puzzle (date, stars, time and streak) on your device. It is only sent to the app or person you choose, using your phone's own sharing options. We never receive it.

Third parties

ReWildoku does not include any third-party services that collect data. Google Play, the store you download ReWildoku from, may process information such as installs and app performance under Google's Privacy Policy. We only see anonymous, combined statistics from Google Play, such as the total number of installs.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your game data stays on your device until you delete it. You can erase it at any time:

  • In the game: Settings, then Reset progress.
  • On your phone: uninstall ReWildoku or clear its app data.

Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, correct or delete personal data held about you, and to complain to a data protection authority. As we do not hold any personal data about ReWildoku players, we will normally have nothing to provide, but you are welcome to contact us at any time. You can also contact the Data Protection Commission, Ireland's data protection authority.

Children

ReWildoku is intended for players aged 13 and over. It does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of ReWildoku adds features that handle personal information, such as Google Play Games leaderboards or optional rewarded ads, we will update this policy before that version is released, change the date at the top, and tell you in the game.
