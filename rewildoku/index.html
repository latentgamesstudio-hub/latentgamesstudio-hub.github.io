ReWildoku

Bring a barren valley back to life, one logic puzzle at a time.

A dry, empty valley has lost its wildlife. With the help of Hazel, a friendly owl, you bring it back. Place one creature in every row, every column and every habitat patch, without any two creatures touching. Every puzzle can be solved by careful thinking alone, no guessing needed.

Restore the Meadow, the Wetlands, the Woodland, the Heath and the Moorland across 200 levels, and watch daisies, bees, rabbits, frogs, otters, hedgehogs, foxes, owls and butterflies return home. There's a new daily puzzle every day, with a trickier challenge at the weekend.

No ads, no account, and no internet needed to play.
