Orebit Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 October 2026 · Applies to Orebit version 1.0 on Android

In short: Orebit does not collect, store or share any personal information. There is no account, no sign-up, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your game progress stays on your phone.

Who we are

Orebit is made by Latent Games, an independent developer based in County Waterford, Ireland ("we", "us"). If you have any question about this policy, email latentgamesstudio@gmail.com.

Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information. Orebit does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, location or any other personal details, and it does not contain advertising, analytics or crash-reporting tools.

The game saves the following on your own device only:

  • Game progress: your ore, upgrades, planets, achievements, collection book, daily rewards and journal.
  • Settings: your sound, theme and accessibility choices.

This information never leaves your device and is never sent to us or to anyone else. Orebit works fully offline.

Sharing your progress

If you tap Share my progress, the game creates an image of your stats on your device. It is only sent to the app or person you choose, using your phone's own sharing options. We never receive it.

Third parties

Orebit does not include any third-party services that collect data. Google Play, the store you download Orebit from, may process information such as installs and app performance under Google's Privacy Policy. We only see anonymous, combined statistics from Google Play, such as the total number of installs.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your game data stays on your device until you delete it. You can erase it at any time:

  • In the game: Settings, then Reset everything, then Erase.
  • On your phone: uninstall Orebit or clear its app data.

Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, correct or delete personal data held about you, and to complain to a data protection authority. As we do not hold any personal data about Orebit players, we will normally have nothing to provide, but you are welcome to contact us at any time. You can also contact the Data Protection Commission, Ireland's data protection authority.

Children

Orebit is intended for players aged 13 and over. It does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of Orebit adds features that handle personal information, such as optional rewarded ads or online leaderboards, we will update this policy before that version is released, change the date at the top, and tell you in the game.
