Orebit

Tap, dig and upgrade your way to the core of Earth, the Moon and Mars.

Start in the topsoil with nothing but a pickaxe and Old Gus, a grumpy miner who has seen it all. Hire miners, build drills and blast through crystal caves, diamond seams and magma chambers to the centre of the Earth. Then take your operation to the Moon, with its day and night cycle and falling meteors, and to Mars, which you terraform as you dig.

Your miners keep working while you're away. No account, no sign-up, and no internet needed to play.
